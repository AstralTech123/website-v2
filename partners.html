Common questions

Asked before you have to ask

Does it cost me anything?

No. There is no fee, no minimum, and no exclusivity. Refer one business or ten or none.

Do I have to sell anything?

No. Make the introduction and we take over. You will never be asked to quote pricing or explain our services.

What if they do not sign up?

Nothing happens. No payment, no penalty, no awkwardness.

When exactly do I get paid?

After the client has been with us for 90 days. That window exists so you are never in the position of having vouched for something that fell apart.

I am a CPA, attorney, or licensed professional. Can I participate?

Often yes, but your own professional conduct rules may require you to disclose referral compensation to your clients, or may restrict accepting it. Please check. And if your rules make the commission awkward, tell us and we will simply refer work to each other instead. Honestly, a two-way referral relationship is usually worth more than a one-time check.

How do I know you will tell me if my referral signs?

We will, in writing, either way. You will also hear from us when the 90 days is up.
